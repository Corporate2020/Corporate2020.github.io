1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Track Gps (Track Gps for Spytec GL300). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with applicable data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Track Gps is a companion utility designed to help users monitor external GPS hardware trackers (such as Spytec GL300, Coban, Sinotrack, Concox, and Teltonika units). We do not require personal account registration (no name, phone number, or email address is required to use the app).

2. Device Permissions & Information We Process

To deliver live telemetry, route history, and safety alerts, the app requests only the minimum necessary Android permissions:

  • Camera Permission: Used strictly on-demand when you choose to scan the 15-digit IMEI barcode printed on your physical GPS hardware unit. Image frames are processed locally in real time on your device using Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning and are never recorded, stored, or uploaded to any server.
  • Location Permission: Optional. Used only while the app is open on screen if you choose to display your phone's current position as a reference point on the map alongside your vehicle's hardware tracker. Your phone's GPS coordinates are never transmitted to or stored on external servers.
  • Network & Internet Access: Used to retrieve telemetry coordinates (latitude, longitude, speed, heading, and timestamps) linked to your bound hardware tracker's IMEI, load OpenStreetMap tiles, and serve advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Foreground Service & Notifications: Used only when you enable real-time Speed Limit Alerts or Geofence Zone Alerts, allowing the app to notify you immediately if your vehicle exceeds your configured speed threshold or exits a designated perimeter.
  • Local Storage: Used solely to cache map tiles and when you explicitly export trip history reports (CSV/JSON) to your device.

3. Advertising & Google AdMob

To keep Track Gps free for all users, the application displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC).

  • Data Collected by AdMob: Google AdMob may automatically collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / AAID), coarse IP-based location, device model, operating system version, and ad interaction data (impressions and clicks) to deliver, measure, and prevent fraud in advertisements.
  • User Control & Opt-Out: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android device settings under Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Google Privacy Policy: For detailed information on how Google processes data in advertising services, please visit https://policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Data Storage, Security & Third-Party Services

  • Local On-Device Storage: Your custom vehicle names, speed thresholds, geofence circles, and cached trip logs are stored locally on your smartphone inside an internal database.
  • Hardware Identifier (IMEI): The 15-digit IMEI number of your GPS tracker is used solely to query your tracker's telemetry stream over encrypted connections.
  • OpenStreetMap (osmdroid): Used to render interactive street and satellite map tiles on your screen.
  • Google ML Kit (On-Device): Used locally for barcode recognition when scanning your tracker's IMEI label.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights (Right to Delete)

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Unlink Hardware Unit: Tapping "Detach Hardware Unit from App" in the Settings screen immediately removes the bound IMEI from your device.
  • Clear Telemetry History: You can delete all cached route points, speed alerts, and geofence logs at any time from the History or Settings screen, or by clearing the app storage in Android Settings.
  • GDPR Rights: Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) have the right to access, rectify, erase, or restrict processing of their data. Contact us via email for any privacy inquiries or server-side telemetry deletion requests.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is a vehicle management utility intended for adult vehicle owners and fleet operators. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 16.

2. Geräteberechtigungen & Verarbeitete Daten

Um Live-Ortung, Routenverläufe und Sicherheitswarnungen bereitzustellen, fordert die App nur die technisch notwendigen Android-Berechtigungen an:

  • Kameraberechtigung: Wird ausschließlich auf Ihren Wunsch verwendet, um den 15-stelligen IMEI-Barcode auf Ihrem GPS-Tracker zu scannen. Die Kamerabilder werden lokal in Echtzeit auf Ihrem Gerät über Google ML Kit verarbeitet und niemals gespeichert oder auf Server hochgeladen.
  • Standortberechtigung: Optional. Wird nur während der aktiven Nutzung der Karte verwendet, falls Sie Ihren eigenen Smartphone-Standort als Referenzpunkt neben Ihrem Fahrzeug-Tracker anzeigen möchten. Der Standort Ihres Smartphones wird niemals an externe Server übertragen oder dort gespeichert.
  • Netzwerk- & Internetzugriff: Erforderlich, um die mit der IMEI Ihres GPS-Trackers verknüpften Koordinaten (Breitengrad, Längengrad, Geschwindigkeit, Richtung und Zeitstempel) abzurufen, Kartenkacheln von OpenStreetMap zu laden und Werbeanzeigen über Google AdMob einzublenden.
  • Vordergrunddienst & Benachrichtigungen: Wird nur verwendet, wenn Sie Geschwindigkeitswarnungen oder Geofence-Zonenwarnungen aktivieren, damit die App Sie sofort benachrichtigen kann, wenn Ihr Fahrzeug das festgelegte Limit überschreitet oder eine Sicherheitszone verlässt.
  • Lokaler Speicher: Wird ausschließlich zum Zwischenspeichern von Kartenkacheln sowie beim manuellen Exportieren von Routenberichten (CSV/JSON) auf Ihr Gerät genutzt.

Um Track Gps für alle Nutzer kostenlos bereitzustellen, blendet die App Werbeanzeigen über den Dienst Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Irland / Google LLC) ein.

  • Hardware-Kennung (IMEI): Die 15-stellige IMEI-Nummer Ihres GPS-Trackers wird ausschließlich verwendet, um die Telemetriedaten Ihres Geräts über verschlüsselte Verbindungen abzurufen.
